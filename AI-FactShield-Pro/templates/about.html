ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built for digital trust.

AI FactShield Pro combines machine-learning signals, media extraction and current-news evidence into one practical verification workflow.

AI / ML

Multimodal

Text, image, PDF, video and audio workflows with graceful fallbacks.

Evidence First

RESPONSIBLE VERIFICATION

Confidence is not certainty.

The platform separates a model signal from an evidence verdict. Claims without strong supporting evidence are marked Unverified rather than being presented as facts. This makes the demo closer to a real information-verification product.
