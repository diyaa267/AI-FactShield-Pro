# Offline Placement Demo

The project contains four offline demo stories. Select any story from the Verification Center, click **Analyze & Verify**, and show the deterministic REAL/FAKE result.

- 2 REAL demo stories backed by the local demo evidence pack.
- 2 deliberately fabricated FAKE stories for demonstrating false-news detection.
- Each story includes a local image and short video preview.
- No Google News, GDELT, or other live-news service is required for the demo.
