ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built for digital trust.

AI FactShield Pro combines machine-learning signals, media extraction and current-news evidence into one practical verification workflow.

AI / ML

TF-IDF/Logistic Regression support plus an explainable signal layer.

Multimodal

Text, image, PDF, video and audio workflows with graceful fallbacks.

Evidence First

Current reporting is shown with source, match score and publication date.

RESPONSIBLE VERIFICATION

Confidence is not certainty.

The platform separates independent evidence from the ML signal. Live APIs and independent sources are checked first; when evidence is inconclusive, the trained model supplies the binary REAL/FAKE fallback so the product always returns one of the two requested outcomes.
