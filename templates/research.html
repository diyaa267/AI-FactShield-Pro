RESEARCH INTERPRETATION

Why the evidence layer matters

The Nadiad pilot benchmark contains verified real reports and documented false Nadiad-attribution claims. The text-only model misses several location-misattributed claims; the controlled evidence replay tests whether source evidence can correct those cases.

ML Only→Evidence Retrieval→Cross-Language Search→Evidence Comparison→REAL / FAKE

Research limitation: the benchmark is small and manually curated. The controlled replay result is not a claim of 100% real-world accuracy.
