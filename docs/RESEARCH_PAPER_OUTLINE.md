### Core research question
Does adding independent, cross-language evidence retrieval improve local misinformation verification compared with a text-only ML classifier?

### Important reporting rule
Do not report the controlled 12-case evidence replay as a 100% real-world accuracy. It is a reproducible component test on a small curated benchmark.
